# Alien Armageddon 5.66 in VR: the weapon and gadget wheels.
# AA has more weapons than weapon slots: several share a slot (curr_weapon) and AA's own
# per-player gamevars say which one is "on top" (AADEFS.CON 4067-4170, AAPLAYER.CON
# EVENT_WEAPKEY2..10 at 21056+, the switch code at 15436-15950, AA's own wheel at 6597+).
# The five characters (pchar: 0 Duke, 1 Bombshell, 2 Wes, 3 Mando, 4 Deanova) use the same
# slots, keys and gamevars; only the shotgun's partner differs (bow for 1 and 2, double barrel
# for 0, 3 and 4). Icons are AA's pickup sprites (AADEFS.CON defines), the same for everyone.
#
# got* bit fields (gotbow, gotDB, gotshock, gotrailgun, gotplasma): 1 = the partner is owned,
# 2 = the partner is on top, 4/8 = switching (cleared when the weapon comes back up, about a
# third of a second after the key). Steady values are 0, 1 and 3.
# A weapon key pressed while its slot is in hand (weapon_pos 0, kickback_pic 0) swaps the pair.

# Slot 0, key 1: kick/fists; the chainsaw (gotsaw, sawammo) and the vorpal blade (gotsaber)
# replace it by themselves, there is nothing to select. 22330 is AA's own wheel icon for melee.
weapon 0 22330 noammo

# Slot 1, key 2 cycles pistol -> alien laser pistol -> commander gun -> pistol (LASERMODE 0, 1, 3;
# negative, 2 and 4 are switching). The laser and commander gun exist while they have rounds.
# The pistol's rounds are ammo_amount[1] while it is on top and pistolammo otherwise (pistolammo
# goes stale while the pistol is put away), so the default is used: with another one on top and
# the pistol empty, picking the pistol presses key 2 in vain.
weapon 1 FIRSTGUNSPRITE is LASERMODE 0
weapon 1 LASERPISTOL key 2 is LASERMODE 1 owned LASERAMMO 0x7fffffff ammo LASERAMMO 100
weapon 1 COMMGUN key 2 is LASERMODE 3 owned COMMAMMO 0x7fffffff ammo COMMAMMO 50

# Slot 2, key 3 swaps shotgun and bow (gotbow, arrows: Bombshell and Wes) or double barrel
# (gotDB, dbammo: Duke, Mando, Deanova). The shotgun's own rounds are ammo_amount[2], which AA
# keeps at 1 when empty. The shotgun is in hand while bit 2 is CLEAR, which this format cannot
# say for a per-character pair (one 'when' per line): it has no 'is', so picking it while the
# bow/double barrel is in hand does nothing. To be replaced by 'isclear gotbow 2' / 'isclear gotDB 2'.
weapon 2 SHOTGUNSPRITE when pchar 1 isclear gotbow 2
weapon 2 SHOTGUNSPRITE when pchar 2 isclear gotbow 2
weapon 2 SHOTGUNSPRITE when pchar 0 isclear gotDB 2
weapon 2 SHOTGUNSPRITE when pchar 3 isclear gotDB 2
weapon 2 SHOTGUNSPRITE when pchar 4 isclear gotDB 2
weapon 2 BOWSPRITE key 3 when pchar 1 isbits gotbow 2 owned gotbow 1 ammo arrows MAXSHOTGUNAMMO
weapon 2 BOWSPRITE key 3 when pchar 2 isbits gotbow 2 owned gotbow 1 ammo arrows MAXSHOTGUNAMMO
weapon 2 DBSPRITE key 3 when pchar 0 isbits gotDB 2 owned gotDB 1 ammo dbammo MAXSHOTGUNAMMO
weapon 2 DBSPRITE key 3 when pchar 3 isbits gotDB 2 owned gotDB 1 ammo dbammo MAXSHOTGUNAMMO
weapon 2 DBSPRITE key 3 when pchar 4 isbits gotDB 2 owned gotDB 1 ammo dbammo MAXSHOTGUNAMMO

# Slot 3, key 4 swaps chaingun and Executioner (gotshock; both use ammo_amount[3]). Two lines
# for the chaingun: without the Executioner (gotshock 0), and with it (in hand while gotshock
# is exactly 1). Wes: key 4 also toggles his dual uzis (wesupgrades[], an array: not listed).
weapon 3 CHAINGUNSPRITE isclear gotshock 2
weapon 3 SHOCKRIFLESPRITE key 4 isbits gotshock 2 owned gotshock 1

# Slot 4, key 5 swaps RPG and nuke (nukeselect 0/1; -1/-2 switching). Nukes are nukeamount at
# all times; rockets are ammo_amount[4] while the RPG is on top and weap4ammo under the nuke.
weapon 4 RPGSPRITE is nukeselect 0
weapon 4 NUKESPRITE key 5 is nukeselect 1 owned nukeamount 0x7fffffff ammo nukeamount 0

# Slot 5, key 6: pipebombs (each character's own grenade). Slot 10 is the detonator.
weapon 5 HEAVYHBOMB

# Slots 6 and 11, key 7 toggles shrinker and expander (the engine's own toggle).
weapon 6 SHRINKERSPRITE
weapon 11 GROWSPRITEICON key 7

# Slot 7, key 8 swaps devastator (Bombshell: grenade launcher, Wes: explosive rounds, Mando and
# Deanova: blaster) and railgun (Mando, Deanova: disintegrator), gotrailgun. Their rounds SWAP:
# ammo_amount[7] is the one on top, railgunammo the other; AA puts the loaded one on top when
# the other is empty, so the default (ammo_amount[7]) is right for selecting; the ring of the
# one underneath shows the other's rounds.
weapon 7 DEVISTATORSPRITE isclear gotrailgun 2
weapon 7 RAILGUNSPRITE key 8 isbits gotrailgun 2 owned gotrailgun 1

# Slot 8, key 9: tripbombs. Spider mines replace them with AA's alternate fire (not a weapon
# key) and are kept in dukeupgrades[] (an array): one entry.
weapon 8 TRIPBOMBSPRITE

# Slot 9, key 10 swaps freezer (or incinerator; Wes: saw launcher) and plasma rifle, gotplasma.
# Rounds swap between ammo_amount[9] and plasmammo like slot 7.
weapon 9 FREEZESPRITE isclear gotplasma 2
weapon 9 PLASMASPRITE key 10 isbits gotplasma 2 owned gotplasma 1

# Usable inventory (AAUSER.CON amounts). Holoduke is each character's own decoy/companion call.
# AA's deployable cover (cover_amount, the Inventory key) is not an inventory slot: not listed.
gadget 9 FIRSTAID MedKit FIRSTAID_AMOUNT
gadget 0 STEROIDS Steroids STEROID_AMOUNT
gadget 3 HOLODUKE Holo_Duke HOLODUKE_AMOUNT
gadget 4 JETPACK Jetpack JETPACK_AMOUNT
gadget 7 HEATSENSOR NightVision HEAT_AMOUNT
